# Friendly Matches and Group Challenges

## Member friendly matches

Friends invite and accept while authenticated. A member's competitive internal MMR is preferred, then normal hidden MMR, then 1000 if neither exists. Problem difficulty uses the mean effective MMR of both members. Friendly results do not change MMR, public points, tier, or XP and do not run automated cheating detection.

## Group challenge creation

An authenticated member creates a challenge from a completed normal or competitive result. The link accepts new participants for three days, has no participant limit, and gives every participant the same five questions completed by the creator.

## Guest entry

- No login or registration is required.
- The guest enters nickname and birth date.
- Nickname availability is checked automatically while typing.
- The same nickname and birth-date identity may attempt a given link once.
- Returning with the completed identity opens the leaderboard instead of a new attempt.
- A different nickname and birth date creates a distinct participant.

## Ranking and disclosure

Participants rank by correct count, then shorter valid solve time. The creator's score and working are released as soon as all participants currently admitted to the challenge have finished; that release is not reversed when someone joins later. Each released participant detail shows actual questions, submitted answers, correctness, time, difficulty-adjusted score, and working.

## Registration and record claim

A guest can propose a reverse challenge from the leaderboard and registers first. Existing guest records link only when nickname, birth-date digest, browser ownership proof, and explicit confirmation of the first participation date all match.
